01 — The technology

A unique process where electrolyte is replaced with a non-corrosive crystalline composite, eliminating maintenance and fire risks.

02 — The benefit

Leadcrystal batteries are engineered for a circular economy, minimising environmental footprint across the entire lifecycle.

A lead crystal battery is an advanced lead-acid chemistry that uses a silica-based electrolyte structure to improve cycle life, deep-discharge tolerance and safety. Compared with conventional flooded lead-acid batteries it offers longer service life, better charge acceptance, lower self-discharge and reduced risk of electrolyte leakage. Compared with AGM and gel it provides the same maintenance-free operation with improved deep-cycle performance.

Built for the Gulf

Rated to +65°C.

Summer temperatures in Saudi Arabia routinely pass 50°C, and equipment enclosures reach 60–65°C. Batteries designed for 25°C can fail in under a year. Lead crystal is rated for continuous operation up to 65°C, with no BMS restrictions.
