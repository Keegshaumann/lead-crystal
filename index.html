Why crystal

Most batteries keep their energy in liquid acid. Ours keep it in crystal. Nothing to spill. Nothing to top up. Nothing to catch fire. Just steady power, in heat that stops other batteries.

01 · The problem

Liquid acid is the weak link.

Conventional flooded lead-acid batteries hold their electrolyte as liquid sulphuric acid. It can spill, vent gas and dry out in high heat, which is why they need ventilation, topping up and early replacement.

  • Spills and corrosion
  • Venting and top-ups
  • Short life in the heat

02 · The breakthrough

We turn it into crystal.

Lead crystal replaces the liquid with a patented SiO₂ (silica) composite. Inside the sealed cell it sets into a stable, non-corrosive crystalline structure.

  • Patented SiO₂ electrolyte
  • Non-corrosive
  • No free liquid

03 · The result

A sealed cell that just works.

Maintenance-free, with no fire or explosion risk. It recovers from a full 0V discharge, recharges up to three times faster than lead acid and runs from −40°C to +65°C.

  • 6,000+ cycles at 20% DoD
  • 0 to 100% in 2.5 hours
  • Up to 18 years float life (2V)

04 · End of life

And 99% of it comes back.

A spent lead crystal battery is non-hazardous and 99% recyclable, so almost all of it can be recovered and reused instead of going to landfill.

  • 99% recyclable
  • Non-hazardous
  • Built for a circular economy

Built for the Gulf

Rated to +65°C.

Summer in Saudi Arabia routinely passes 50°C, and equipment enclosures reach 60 to 65°C. Batteries designed for 25°C can fail in under a year. Lead crystal is rated for continuous operation up to 65°C.
