Our story

Thirty years from an idea to a battery in service around the world.

Lead crystal began with an invention in the 1990s: a composite electrolyte that replaces the sulfuric acid in a lead battery. Lead Crystal Technologies took it from the lab to commercial products and then to large-scale production, and today researches, develops, makes and sells the full lead crystal range.

The patented SiO₂-based electrolyte forms a crystalline structure as the battery charges. It holds the active material on the plates and keeps the ions moving freely, which is why our batteries last longer, cope with heat and cold, lose little charge on the shelf and recover from deep discharge.

Renewable energy, telecoms, vehicles, rail and industry all run on it now, often in places where other batteries fail first. Leadcrystal brings that range to Saudi Arabia and the Gulf, with stock, sizing advice and support from Riyadh.

Performance

What the chemistry does for you.

  • 0V recovery

    Back to 100% or more of rated capacity within 10 cycles, even after a full discharge to 0V.

  • Low charging voltage

    2.35 to 2.40V per cell at 20 to 30°C, with charge acceptance of 0.35C10.

  • Cold and heat

    85% or more of rated capacity at -10°C, and still 40% or more at -40°C.

  • No stratification

    The crystalline electrolyte stays evenly spread, so every cell performs the same.

  • Long service life

    Up to 15 years in service under the right conditions.

  • Non-dangerous goods

    No free sulfuric acid, so no leaks in use or in transport.
