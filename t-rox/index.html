Two chemistries, one family

AGM or GEL?

Both are VRLA: valve-regulated lead-acid, the category for every sealed, maintenance-free lead-acid battery. The difference is how the electrolyte is held, and that decides where each one works best.

Fast · powerful · responsive

AGM Absorbent glass mat

The electrolyte is absorbed in fibreglass mats, so the battery recharges quickly, delivers high-rate discharge and shrugs off vibration.

  • Fast recharge
  • High-rate discharge
  • Vibration resistant

Best for UPS, engine starting and dynamic loads

Steady · resilient · thermal control

GEL Silica gel electrolyte

The electrolyte is held in a silica gel, which tolerates heat better, discharges deeper and gives up to 30% longer cycle life than standard AGM.

  • Superior heat tolerance
  • Deeper discharge
  • Longer cycle life

Best for Extreme temperatures, deep cycling and renewable storage
